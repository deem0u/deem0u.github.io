The following contact page QR codes are for:

Contact Page URL — Print and stick this as is or import this to your chosen NFC app to write to a tag. When the QR is scanned or NFC is tapped it will redirect to your Contact Page URL.

vCard — Print and stick this as is or import this to your chosen NFC app to write to a tag. When the QR is scanned or NFC is tapped it will download on the user's phone as a vCard/phone contact which can then be saved.

Putting both on one NFC tag: You can store both a URL and a vCard on the same NFC tag. How a phone reacts depends on the device: Android usually offers to save the contact if the vCard is first; iPhones often work better with the URL (e.g. a link to download the contact) and may not handle the vCard directly. If you put both on one tag, expect that some phones will prefer one or the other. You can still generate these QR codes later from My Account.
