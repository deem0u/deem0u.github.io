Frequently Asked Questions

What is DigiCon iD?

DigiCon iD is a digital luggage tag system. You get a personal contact page and a QR code. When someone scans the code on your tag, they can reach you without seeing your private details on the tag itself.

How do I get my contact page and QR code?

Sign up or sign in via My Account. You can create and edit your contact page there and download or copy your QR code and link to print or share.

Who can see my contact information?

Only people who open your unique link or scan your QR code see your contact page. You choose what to show (e.g. a contact form, email, or other details) and can change it anytime in My Account.

Is my data secure?

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data. For more detail, see our Terms of Use & Privacy Policy.

I lost my luggage—what should I do?

Report it to the airline or transport provider as usual. If someone finds your bag and scans your DigiCon iD tag, they can use your contact page to get in touch with you.

Where can I learn more about QR codes?

See our Guide to QR Codes for a short overview of how QR codes work and how to use them on your tag.
