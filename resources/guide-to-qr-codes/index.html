Guide to QR Codes

QR codes on your DigiCon iD luggage tag link to your digital contact page. Here’s how they work and how to use them.

What is a QR code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a 2D barcode that can store a URL or other data. When someone scans it with their phone camera or a QR app, they’re taken straight to that link—in our case, your contact page.

Why use one on a luggage tag?

Instead of printing your name, phone number, or address on the tag, you only show a QR code. Finders can scan it to reach your contact page and get in touch without seeing your private details until you choose to share them.

How to scan a QR code

Most modern smartphones can scan QR codes with the built-in camera: open the camera app, point it at the code, and tap the notification that appears. If that doesn’t work, you can install a free QR code reader from your device’s app store.

Printing and durability

Print your tag on a sturdy material and, if possible, laminate or sleeve it so the QR code stays clear and readable after handling and travel.
