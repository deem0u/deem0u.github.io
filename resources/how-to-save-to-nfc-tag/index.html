How to save to an NFC Tag

This guide walks you through writing your DigiCon iD contact page (or vCard) to an NFC tag using your phone. You don’t have to type anything—scan the QR from My Account inside an NFC app and tap the tag to write it.

What you need

  • A phone with NFC (most recent Android and iPhone models have it).
  • An NFC tag—sticker, card, or keychain (for both URL and vCard on one tag, use a higher-capacity type like NTAG216).
  • An NFC app that can write tags and read QR codes or accept a URL. We recommend NFC Tools (wakdev) or NFC TagWriter by NXP—both are free on iOS and Android.
  • Your DigiCon iD QR code or contact page URL from My Account (open your contact page, then QR Codes).

Step-by-step: write to your tag

  1. Get your QR or URL. Sign in at My Account, open your contact page, and go to QR Codes. You can use the Contact Page URL (opens your page in a browser) or the vCard (saves you as a contact). Have the QR on screen or copy the URL.
  2. Install an NFC app if you haven’t already—e.g. NFC Tools or NFC TagWriter by NXP from the App Store or Google Play.
  3. Open the app and start a “Write” or “Add record” flow. Look for an option to add a URL or vCard (or “Contact”).
  4. Scan the QR or paste the URL. Many apps let you scan a QR code to fill in the URL or vCard automatically—point your phone at the QR shown in My Account. If the app doesn’t support scanning, use Copy Contact Page URL (or copy the vCard) in My Account and paste it into the app.
  5. Tap your phone to the NFC tag. Hold the back of your phone against the tag and keep it there until the app says the write succeeded. Avoid moving the phone or tag during the write.

Tip: You can write the same URL or vCard to multiple tags—generate once in My Account, then scan or paste in the app and tap each tag in turn.

Putting both URL and vCard on one tag

If your app and tag support it, you can store both your contact page URL and your vCard on the same NFC tag. Use a tag with enough capacity (e.g. NTAG216). Add one record as a URL and one as a vCard in the app, then write to the tag. How a finder’s phone reacts depends on the device: some may offer to open the link, others to save the contact. For more on this, see What are QR Codes and NFC Tags.

Troubleshooting

Write failed or “tag too small”: Your tag may not have enough memory. Use a larger-capacity tag (e.g. NTAG216) or write only the URL or only the vCard.

Phone doesn’t detect the tag: Ensure NFC is turned on in your phone settings. Hold the back of the phone flat against the tag and keep it still for a few seconds. Try moving the tag to different spots on the back of the phone (NFC antenna location varies by model).

Finder’s phone doesn’t open the link or contact: Behaviour varies by device and OS. The contact page URL works in a browser; the vCard is for saving a contact. See What are QR Codes and NFC Tags for more on how different phones handle NFC.
