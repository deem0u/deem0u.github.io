How It Works

1. Create your account

On the Home page, click Get Started and sign up with your email. You’ll choose a user name and set a password. Once your account is created, you can sign in anytime from My Account.

2. Your Digital Contact Page

Each account has a public contact page that shows the details you enter: your name, contact info, destination details, and any extra information you add. You can have one main page (index) and optionally more pages (e.g. for different trips or purposes).

3. Edit and update

Sign in at My Account to add or change your contact details. You can update your page whenever you like. Changes are saved and appear on your live page straight away.

4. Share your link

Your page has a unique URL (for example https://deem0u.github.io/user/yourname/). Share this link with family, friends, or anyone who needs to reach you. You can use it on luggage tags, in emails, or on social profiles.
